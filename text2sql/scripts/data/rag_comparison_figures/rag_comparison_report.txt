OVERALL SUMMARY
================================================================================

Key Findings:

1. RAG Impact on Accuracy:
   - RAG consistently improves Execution Accuracy across all techniques
   - The improvement is most significant for complex queries

2. Technique Performance:
   - Execution-Guided (EG) shows the highest accuracy with RAG
   - Few-Shot benefits most from RAG in terms of EX improvement

3. Latency Trade-offs:
   - RAG adds minimal latency overhead
   - The accuracy gains justify the slight latency increase

4. Recommendations:
   - Use RAG for production Text-to-SQL systems
   - Combine RAG with Execution-Guided technique for best results
